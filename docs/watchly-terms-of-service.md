## 3. Rooms, Content, and Conduct

### 3.1 Creating and Joining Rooms
- Any user can create a room, set it public or private (password-protected), and choose
  whether it's visible to their friends.
- The room creator is automatically the room's **admin**, with control over playback,
  room membership, and moderation (see 3.3).
- Rooms are joined via a unique code, an accepted friend-discovery request, or an
  admin-sent invite.

### 3.2 Video Content
- In its current version, Watchly does not host video files — you can only bring in
  video via a link to a third-party platform (e.g. YouTube, Vimeo) or a direct video URL.
- **We don't control, endorse, or take responsibility for third-party content** played
  through links in a Watchly room. Playing a video link doesn't mean we've reviewed or
  approved it.
- You agree not to share links to content that is illegal, infringes someone else's
  copyright, or that you don't have the right to share.
- If we receive a valid copyright or legal takedown request related to content shared
  through the Service, we may remove access to the relevant room or restrict the
  account involved.

### 3.3 Room Admin Powers
- Room admins can mute, kick, or ban participants from their own room, and can transfer
  admin status to another participant.
- Admin actions apply only within that admin's own room — they don't give one user
  control over another user's account or data outside that room.
- We are not responsible for how individual room admins choose to moderate their rooms,
  but we may step in at the platform level for violations of these Terms (e.g. harassment
  reports, abuse of the Service).

### 3.4 Chat and Voice
- Text chat in a room is **ephemeral** — it is not stored by us and disappears when the
  room closes or you disconnect. We don't retain a record of what was said in chat.
- Voice chat is transmitted directly between participants (peer-to-peer) and is not
  recorded or stored by us.
- You're responsible for what you say in a room's chat or voice channel. Don't use
  Watchly to harass, threaten, or abuse other users.

### 3.5 Friends
- Adding a friend requires mutual acceptance. Being someone's friend on Watchly lets them
  see your public live rooms (if you've enabled that) and vice versa.
- You can unfriend someone at any time, which removes that mutual visibility.
